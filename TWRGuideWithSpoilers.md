Ealry game - unarmed ship stage

The cheapest vuiable ship design is ship with science console and a shuttle that can scan for sites, then visit sites using thaat shuttle and mine/deconsrutc stuff for resources.That is actually quite hard to begin with. But that is doable.
There is no safe CR, even ships with lowerest CR will be attacked by small fighters. Unless ship battle frequency isn't set to 0 in mod options. Fastest enemy fighter has TRW of 4.1. So to be able to run away from them on non-combat ship, need to have TWR of 4.2 or better.
Minimal ship needs to have:
1. Pilot console. Pilot console is 1x3=3 tile and AI/droone core is 3x3=9 tiles. Pilot console is much better, as weight is crucial for small ships.
2. One or 2 science consoles for scanning for space locations. Large Sensors is larger than science console, also they scan rather slow, so console is the absolutely preferred building here.
3. Power source. Replacing a few tiles of outer ship hull woth solar panels doesn't add weigth. While reactor is 3x3 tiles, adds quite some. Solar panels are absolute winner. Will need to add 2x1 tiles small ship capacitor. That should be enough to cover power need during night.
4. Engines. For a very small ship, 1 or 2 large engines. Nuclear engine is a little more effcient, so if starter ship can't have 4.2 TWR with 2 large engines, nuclear engine is recommended.
5. Beds if sleep need is not negated by DLC or mod features.
6. Food source, also table and chair to eat at. One hydroponics takes 4 tiles, while even with Vanilla storage can store 120 packaged survuival meals in the same 4 tiles. Enough for 30 days for a crew of 2 pawns eating 2 meals per day. So shelves with packaged survival meals are preferred. Table and chair can be omitted if pawns cant tank eat without table mood debuff.
7. Recreation. May take some chocolate, use chess table/game-of-ur board. May use harp. Don't need many recreation types, as wealth of the early game small ship will be quite low.
8. Way to gather resources from derelicts/satellites/asteroids. The most lightweight solution is Personal shuttle parked on 1x1 hardpoint. May be placed outside the ship or in a separate room, as harpoint is unroofed, so room containing it will be exposed to vacuum an have -100C temperature.
9. A few free shelves. To store gathered materials somewere before starting ship upgrade.
10. Extra fuel. Fleeing from enemy attacks will burn fuel, shuttle runs will do that too. 

What extras can be included:
1. Fancy floors won't add weight. But will boost beauty insde ship, that will help with keeping crew mood high.

What should be avoided.
1. As mentioned above, food production.
2. As mentioned above, AI/Drone core and advanced scanners.
3. Spaceship is very different from Gravship. TWR is important, while for gravship it doesn't matter much if it occupies 280 or 500 tiles, only affects space required for landing. So copying gravship into early-game unarmes SOS 2 ship won't work. Will be too heavy to retreat form enemy fighters.
4. Large ship buildings such as 5x5 Shuttle bay or Salvage bay, those add a lot of weight.
5. Basically, anything. Extra workbenches, separate bedroom, etc.
