Glossary

TWR
	Ship thrust to weight ratio. Calculated as sum of all ship engine thrust values divided by sum of all parts mass. When player or enemy has multiple ships on map, they can advance or retreat with the speed of the slowerest ship in the fleet. 
	
Bridge
	Ship pilot console and AI or Drone core are called bridges.
	
PD
	Point defense. Small lasers and small cannons can be swithced to point defense mode when they don't shoot at enemy ship but target incoming enemy torpedoes and shuttles. Cannon is notable better than laser in PD role due to much faster reload, but it requires refueling with uranium.
