Torpedoes and point defense

Small lasers and small cannons can be swithced to point defense mode when they don't shoot at enemy ship but target incoming enemy torpedoes and shuttles. Cannon is notable better than laser in PD role due to much faster reload, but it requires refueling with uranium. Enemies will use PD-capable weapons as point defense automatically. But also will use those as ship-to ship weapons. So it's possible to approach enemy ship, get enemy PD-capable weapons shoot at player ship and go on cooldown, them launch some torpedoes tat couldn't be intercepted.
	
Point defense weapons have FIRE RATE LIMITATION: about 5.5 shots per second per map is the cap. That means whatever humber of PD weapons are available on current map, thay won't shoot more often than 5.5 PD shots per second. So 5.5 multiplied by cooldown time is the effective PD weapons cap, there is no need to build 200 small cannons.

Enemy ships usually decently well equipped with point defense weapons according to their class. So it is NOT RECOMMENDED to use a few torpedo launcehrs as auxillary weapons. For example, Eneny Corvette Gunship will have 10 small lasers. If there are 2 torpedo launchers on player ship as auxillary weapons, they will do nothing, torpedoes will be shot down by enemy PD. Unless the latter are disabled somehow, which is rather advanced tactic.  Maybe by advancing to small laser range so that those small lasers fire at player ship and can't shoot at incoming torpedoes while on cooldown. Or sending expendable shuttle to take PD fire before shooting torpedo launchers. Or shooting topredo launchers when enemy is doing active heat venting and PDs can't shoot.
	
Recommanded way of using torpedoes would be either building a ship very focused on/dedicated to torpedo launchers, so that enemy PDs are overwhelmed by swarms of incoming torpedoes. Or notusing torpedoes at all.
	
HE Torpedoes deal abot 1150 damage to shields. When hitting hull, the esplosion is powerful enough to destroy one layer of hull in 6 tiles raduis.
EMP torpedoes do about 1500 shield damage. When hitting ship, they don't damage buildings but will stun exposed enemy weapons.
Antigrain torpedoes deal abot 11500 damage to shield. When hit hull rather than shield, one Antigrain torpedo hit causes muliple explosions.
