Formgels

Afterlife casket can spawn a formgel of a dead pawn. See afterlife casket description for more details.
Ship AI core can spawn AI formgel.

AI formgels have a base of 16 bandwidth. They are mechanitor-capable, but can't install mechanitor upgrade implants. But have certain bonuses to their mechanitor capabilities to make up for that.
"Dead" formgel can be respawned in 24 hours.
Xenogerms can be implanted into formgels, they will remain after respawn. Bionics can be installed on formgelds, but they will drop on the ground if formgel "dies".
