Ship mass

Pipes, hull plating and attachments have a mass of 1 per occupied tile.
Buildings, including hip hull have a mass of 3 per tile. So, 2x1 table weights 6 units. Smaller 2x2 shiip weapons weight 12 units.
Items like resources and uninstalled buildings don't add to ship weight.
Pawns don't add to ship weight.
Vehicle cargo doesn't add to ship weight.
Engines have a very high mass of 60 units per tile. So, large 3x3 thruser weights 540 uints. Nuclear engine weights 1500 units.

At the end of ship mass calculation, it is raised into 1power of 1.2, in other words, multiplied by fifth root of itself. That means that the larger the ship, the slower it is. Which means that if ship size is incresed twice and numver of engines is increased twice too, the enlarged variant of the ship will have about 15% more mass comapred to just doubled mass of original ship.  Thst will result in about 15% less TWR.
